04 / Token Economy

Revenue connected to the native token

Arc Forge intends to connect platform growth with the long term development of its native token through a planned revenue based buyback and burn mechanism.

As Arc Forge generates revenue, a portion of that revenue is planned to be used to purchase the project's native token from the market and subsequently burn those tokens.

Platform Revenue → Token Buyback → Token Burn

The exact allocation, execution method, and schedule will be disclosed as the token launch approaches.
